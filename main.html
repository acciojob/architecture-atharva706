Architecture Through Time

Modern Architecture

Modern architecture focuses on simplicity, functionality, and innovative materials. Glass, steel, and concrete are commonly used to create clean and efficient structures.

Large windows, open spaces, and minimal decoration are important features of modern architectural design.

Traditional Architecture

Traditional architecture represents the culture, history, and environment of a particular region. Historic buildings often contain detailed craftsmanship and local materials.

Temples, forts, palaces, and historic homes demonstrate how architecture preserves cultural identity.
